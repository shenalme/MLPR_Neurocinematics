Paper under review

Predicting shared neural responses to cinematic content

Notebook, data files and supplementary material for the paper. The study predicts audience brain synchrony (EEG inter-subject correlation) from interpretable film features, using recordings from nine viewers of a 24-minute film.

Technologies used

Version numbers are those recorded in run_info.json.

  • PythonLanguage for the whole pipeline.
  • MNE-Python 1.13.2EEG preprocessing: channel mapping, interpolation, referencing, filtering, resampling, EOG regression.Gramfort et al., Front. Neurosci., 2013
  • OpenCVFrame-level visual features, optical flow and shot-cut detection.
  • FFmpegVideo decoding and audio extraction.
  • scikit-learn 1.6.1Logistic regression, SVM, random forest, MLP, PCA and evaluation metrics.Pedregosa et al., J. Mach. Learn. Res., 2011
  • XGBoost 3.4.1Gradient-boosted tree classifier.Chen and Guestrin, KDD, 2016
  • SHAP 0.52.0Global, dependence and local model explanations.Lundberg and Lee, NeurIPS, 2017
  • PyTorchRuntime for the image embedding models.
  • CLIP ViT-B/32512-dimensional image embeddings, sampled at 1 fps.Radford et al., ICML, 2021
  • DINOv2 ViT-S/14384-dimensional image embeddings, sampled at 1 fps.Oquab et al., Trans. Mach. Learn. Res., 2024
  • Hugging Face TransformersLoading the pretrained DINOv2 model (facebook/dinov2-small).
