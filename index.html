Predicting shared neural responses to cinematic content

Senal Dharmasiri, Wai Keung Fung, Shadan Khan Khattak and Esyin Chew Eureka Robotics Center, Cardiff Metropolitan University, Cardiff, UK

Code, data and citation details for the paper. Using EEG from nine viewers of a 24-minute film, the study predicts inter-subject correlation (audience brain synchrony) from interpretable film features under a leakage-controlled, leave-one-subject-out evaluation.
