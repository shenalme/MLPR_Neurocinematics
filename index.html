Predicting shared neural responses to cinematic content

Code, data and supplementary material for the paper. The study predicts audience brain synchrony (EEG inter-subject correlation) from interpretable film features, using recordings from nine viewers of a 24-minute film.

      Dataset

      • KU Leuven Video-EEG Encoding–Decoding dataset

        Source EEG and stimulus information. The study uses the Mr. Bean subset: nine participants watching a 24-minute excerpt without sound, recorded with a 64-channel BioSemi ActiveTwo system at 2048 Hz.

        Yao, Y., Stebner, A., Tuytelaars, T., et al., Zenodo, 2024, doi: 10.5281/zenodo.10512414

        Described in: Yao, Y., Stebner, A., Tuytelaars, T., et al., ‘Identifying temporal correlations between natural single-shot videos and EEG signals’, J. Neural Eng., 2024, 21, (1), 016018

        Open on Zenodo

      Technologies used

      • PythonLanguage for the whole pipeline.
      • MNE-PythonEEG preprocessing: channel mapping, interpolation, referencing, filtering, resampling, EOG regression.Gramfort et al., Front. Neurosci., 2013
      • OpenCVFrame-level visual features, optical flow and shot-cut detection.
      • FFmpegVideo decoding and audio extraction.
      • scikit-learnLogistic regression, SVM, random forest, MLP, PCA and evaluation metrics.Pedregosa et al., J. Mach. Learn. Res., 2011
      • XGBoostGradient-boosted tree classifier.Chen and Guestrin, KDD, 2016
      • SHAPGlobal, dependence and local model explanations.Lundberg and Lee, NeurIPS, 2017
      • PyTorchRuntime for the image embedding models.
      • CLIP ViT-B/32512-dimensional image embeddings, sampled at 1 fps.Radford et al., ICML, 2021
      • DINOv2 ViT-S/14384-dimensional image embeddings, sampled at 1 fps.Oquab et al., Trans. Mach. Learn. Res., 2024
      • Hugging Face TransformersLoading the pretrained embedding models.
